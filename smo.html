The Fundamentals

Essential Movement Tech

Before attempting skips, you must master Mario's momentum.

Roll Cancel

Concept: Cancels the rolling animation to instantly gain max speed or redirect momentum.

Inputs: Press Y (Roll) then immediately B (Jump).

Use Case: Crossing gaps without losing speed and preserving momentum from slopes.

Vectoring

Concept: Turning Mario 45° mid-air confuses the game engine, adding speed to his trajectory.

Inputs: Rotate analog stick 45° away from destination, then correct back.

Use Case: Extending jump distance significantly to bypass intended platforms.

Cap Dive

Concept: Using Cappy as a temporary platform to gain height or distance.

Inputs: Y (Throw) → Hold Y → ZL+Y (Dive).

Use Case: Essential for almost every skip in the game, including scaling walls.

Major Run-Saving Skips

These tricks separate the casual runners from the world record holders.

Dino Skip Skip (DSS)

CASCADE KINGDOM

The T-Rex is Too Slow

Originally, runners captured the T-Rex to smash blocks and use a trampoline ("Dino Skip"). Dino Skip Skip optimizes this by ignoring the dinosaur entirely. Runners perform a precise series of wall jumps and cap dives on a tiny piece of collision on the cliffside to reach Madame Broode directly.

  • Difficulty: Very High
  • Time Save: ~8-9 seconds over standard Dino Skip

Nut Clip

WOODED KINGDOM

Breaking Bounds

By grabbing a specific Nut and performing a precise jump/dive sequence near a corner, Mario can clip through the wall. This allows runners to swim out of bounds and bypass a significant portion of the "Road to Sky Garden" objective.

  • Difficulty: High (Precise Setup)
  • Time Save: Significant detour avoidance

Moon Cave Skip

MOON KINGDOM

Skipping the Finale

In the final kingdom, a long 2D lava section usually awaits. Runners bypass this by utilizing the Moon's low gravity. By performing a specific long jump and wall jump off the Sphynx or nearby architecture, Mario can ascend the outside of the cliff and trigger the final Bowser fight immediately.

  • Difficulty: Medium/High
  • Time Save: Skips entire cave section
